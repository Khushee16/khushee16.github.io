💅 Manicures

Natural nails, gel and dip — from a quick tidy-up to a full set.

Classic ManicureCall for price

The everyday reset for natural nails.

Includes: nail shaping & filing, cuticle care, light hand massage, your choice of regular polish.

Gel ManicureCall for price

Our most popular manicure — color that stays glossy for weeks, not days.

Includes: everything in Classic, plus gel polish cured for a hard, chip-resistant, high-shine finish. Huge color library to choose from.

Dip Powder ManicureCall for price

Strong, natural-feeling nails — a salon favorite for durability.

Includes: shaping & cuticle care, dip powder application in your color, smooth sealed finish. Great if gel alone doesn't last on your nails.

Acrylic Full SetCall for price

Length and shape, built from scratch.

Includes: tips/extensions, acrylic application, your shape — square, coffin, almond, round or stiletto — and regular or gel polish finish.

Refill / FillCall for price

Keep an acrylic or dip set fresh between full appointments.

Includes: grown-out area rebalanced & refilled, reshaping, repair of lifted areas, fresh color.

Polish Change — HandsCall for price

Just want a new color? Quick in-and-out service.

Includes: old polish removed, light shaping, new regular polish.

Gel PedicureCall for price
